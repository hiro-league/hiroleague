# Cousin Ahmed Visiting

My cousin Ahmed came up from Cairo for the weekend. He's Aunt Salma's son —
so he's mom's nephew on her side.

We went to the museum on Saturday and had lunch at a small place near the
river. Ahmed's still working at the law firm in downtown Cairo; he made
partner last year, which the whole family is proud of.

Aunt Salma keeps telling Ahmed to come visit more often. He promised he'd
try to make it for Lina's birthday in October.
