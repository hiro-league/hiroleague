# My Family

I'm Maya. This is what I want to remember about my family.

My mother Sara lives in Cairo with my dad. Mom worries about everyone but she's
the heart of the family. We talk every Sunday on the phone, sometimes for hours.

My sister Lina married Omar in March 2021. The wedding was small — just family.
Lina and Omar live in Cairo too, in a flat not far from mom's place. They have
two kids now.

Aunt Salma — mom's older sister — visits often. She's been a second mother to
Lina and me ever since we were little. Salma lives a few streets away from mom.
