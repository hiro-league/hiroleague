# Ski Trip — Chamonix, February 2023

The 2023 ski trip was unforgettable. Lina, Aunt Salma, and I spent a week
in Chamonix in February.

Lina is much better on skis than I am — she'd grown up skiing during the
years she lived in Switzerland after college. Aunt Salma stayed mostly in
the chalet reading and drinking hot chocolate, which honestly sounded
better than most of my runs.

It snowed every day. We took the cable car up to Aiguille du Midi on the
last morning — the view of Mont Blanc was the highlight of the whole trip.
