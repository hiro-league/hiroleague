# Paris Trip — July 2025

In July 2025, I traveled to Paris with Selim. We stayed in a small hotel
near the Eiffel Tower for a week.

Selim is a coworker — he and I both work at Acme, on the design side. He
suggested the trip after we wrapped up the Q2 launch and both of us needed
the time off. We walked everywhere, ate too much, and barely opened our
laptops the whole week.

We didn't try to fit in everything. Two slow mornings at a café in Saint-
Germain, an afternoon at the Musée d'Orsay, a long evening by the Seine —
that was the trip.
